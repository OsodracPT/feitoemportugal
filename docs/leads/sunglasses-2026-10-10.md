### fora — https://fora.pt
Own site states only "DESIGNED IN LISBON" / "based in Lisbon, Portugal", but production
in Portugal is established by the founder and press:
- Founder Miguel Barral, Time Out interview: "Mais do que uma marca portuguesa, somos
  uma marca feita em Portugal."
- Same interview: production was moved from Italy to a factory in the north of Portugal
  (14 people); the Rua da Prata (Baixa) store has a workshop where lenses are cut and
  fitted into frames.
- Inputs not Portuguese: Italian acetate, Carl Zeiss lenses (sourcing, not a scope
  breaker). Scope `total`.
Own shops (no street addresses published, so no `physical_stores` yet): Lisbon — Chiado,
Campo de Ourique, Rua da Prata; Porto — Mouzinho. IG fora_sunglasses.
Note for maintainer: the production claim is a founder interview, not the brand's own
site copy — confirm before publishing; a `location` (factory "north of Portugal",
town unnamed) was not set.
