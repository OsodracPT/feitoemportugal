## Findings
- **atelier-do-sabao** — contradicts "produzimos tudo à mão": the shop also sells dishwasher
  tablets, essential and vegetable oils, toothpaste, stone and marble soap dishes. The
  handmade soaps are fine; scope looks `parcial`.
- **ambar** — the factory claim stands ("Fábrica: Rua Manuel Pinto de Azevedo … Porto"), but
  the shop also sells third-party goods (Pip Studio pouches, thermal bottles). Weak
  case for `parcial`.
- **boxpt** — gym equipment (spinning bikes, bumpers, knee sleeves of the "Hard 2 Kill" line).
  Only claim: founder quote "a Portuguese brand manufactured in Portugal"
  (https://eu.boxpt.com/en). No factory named; bikes and sleeves very likely imported.
  Candidate for `parcial` or back to draft.
- **hali-studio** — the shop mixes Hali Studio pieces with a second label, "2o3"
  (sweatshirts, t-shirts). Origin of 2o3 not checked.
- **azeite-caixeiro** — no online shop; sales only at "nossa pequena loja" at the SORESA
  head office, Rua do Prado 1, 5445-052 Stª Maria de Émeres (Valpaços). `online_store`
  should go; the shop at the head office needs a decision.
- **ferreira-de-sa** — catalogue only, "produzidos em Portugal com técnicas artesanais";
  no factory named on the home page.
- **dancers-by-georgia** — "transformadas no nosso atelier", Rua Lucinda Simões 12 E,
  1900-068 Lisboa (atelier, not a shop). Upcycled vintage. Products not set.
- **dois-corvos** — brewery Av. Infante D. Henrique 306, Armazém 5, Lisboa; shop moved to
  https://shop.doiscorvos.pt. Taprooms added.
- **compal** — home page names no factory; Carnaxide address is the head office. No change.
- **carolina-machado, citadin** — large share of the range on sale (end of season).
