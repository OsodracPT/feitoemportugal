# Research pass, 2026-11-19

Doghammer, suggested by the maintainer, and a new `barefoot` tag so that a search for
"barefoot" finds the brands that make that kind of shoe.

## doghammer (new, published)

Verdict: strong for production; a foreign brand whose whole shoe range is made in Portugal.

- "Seit 2018 arbeiten wir mit einer familiengeführten Schuhmanufaktur im Norden
  Portugals zusammen" ; "Unsere Manufaktur liegt nur eine Autostunde von Porto
  entfernt" ; "All unserer Schuhe und Barfußschuhe werden in Portugal gefertigt." —
  https://www.doghammer.de/en/handcrafted
- Independent: utopia.de, "Die Schuhe des nachhaltigen Unternehmens werden in
  Handarbeit in einer Schuhmanufaktur in Portugal hergestellt." —
  https://utopia.de/produkt/doghammer/ . It also says a Rosenheim workshop makes
  custom toe-post sandals; the brand's page now says all shoes are made in Portugal.
  `scope: total` for now; revisit if the Rosenheim line is still sold.
- Founded 2015: a Schwan-STABILO press release (investor since 2022) names the
  founders and 2015; the earliest Wayback capture of doghammer.de is 2015-03-27.
- Start of the Portugal partnership: the site says 2018 on one page; other material
  mentions summer 2017. Not used in the YAML.
- Factory not named, only "about an hour from Porto", so no `location`.
- `vegano` left out: the range includes felt and ski-skin slippers, and nothing
  confirms every model is animal-free.
- Published by the maintainer on 2026-11-19. Still to add: logo and photos (via `scripts/apply-media.ts`),
  prices, where to buy.

## barefoot tag

Added to `tags.yaml` (Estilo group), with synonyms for "minimalist shoes", "zero drop"
and so on. Applied where the brand's own site sells shoes as barefoot:

- `mukishoes`: "sustainable barefoot shoes made in Portugal" — https://mukishoes.com
- `lazuli`: a "Barefoot" category with the myBAREFOOT line — https://www.lazuli.pt
- `newve`: products labelled barefoot; "flexible Sohle und breite Zehenbox" —
  https://newve.pt
- `vibae`: tagged at the maintainer's call (2026-11-19). Its FAQ places it in "the
  minimalist footwear arena", with "thin soles, that are light and flexible, and we
  have a wide toebox", though "not barefoot in traditional terms" because of its
  footbed — https://vibae.com
- `doghammer`.

Checked and not tagged: `hirundo` ("minimalist" is about the look only), `zas-tras` (soft baby
booties, not sold as barefoot).

## Second batch: three barefoot brands suggested by the maintainer

### blusun (new, published)

Verdict: strong; a foreign brand whose shoes are made in Portugal.

- "The manufacturing of our soles takes place in Pirmasens and the complete shoe is
  produced in Portugal." ; "Our shoes are made in specialised family businesses in
  Portugal that we visit in person on a regular basis." —
  https://blusun.shoes/en/blogs/news/sustainable-barefoot-shoes
- Home page: "Premium barefoot shoes made in Portugal" — https://blusun.shoes/en
- Independent: FashionUnited interview (2025-09-01), "Wir produzieren in Portugal";
  everything except the lasts is made there; founded January 2024, first
  collection May 2024; about 95 retailers —
  https://fashionunited.de/nachrichten/business/barfussschuhmarke-blusun-vor-15-jahren-gab-es-drei-mitbewerber-heute-sind-es-150/2025090163196
- `founded: 2024`: the interview, plus the earliest Wayback capture of blusun.shoes
  (2024-05-23).
- BLUSUN GmbH, Pirmasens. The Portuguese factories are not named, so no `location`.
- The sole is made in Germany but the shoe is made in Portugal, so `scope: total`.
- Regular prices €99.90–199.90 (home page, 2026-11-19); not sampled into
  `prices.csv` yet. Logo and photos still to add.

### ohne-project (lead, not listed)

Verdict: contradicts. Spanish brand (Barcelona) making most of its range in Spain.

- "All OHNE PROJECT shoes are handcrafted in Elche, Spain" and the LUMA loafer
  ("Designed in Barcelona, handcrafted in Elche"), according to search snippets
  of ohneproject.com. The site returned 429 or an empty body to direct fetches.
- Some models are made in Portugal: the Nalu sandal ("Made in Portugal") and the
  Zuri Chelsea boot ("Handmade in Portugal"). Modaes reports production split between
  Elche and Portugal by product type —
  https://www.modaes.com/empresas/ohne-una-nueva-vision-del-universo-barefoot-apunta-a-los-42-millones-de-euros
- Most of the range is made in Spain, so it fails the foreign-brand rule.

### shapen (lead, not listed)

Verdict: weak. Slovak brand (Bratislava); production in Portugal and Poland, share
unknown.

- "I decided to relocate production to Portugal." ; "Additionally, we produce some
  of our models in Poland" — https://www.shapenbarefoot.com/en/our-story
- Product pages say only "Made: in the European Union" (for example
  https://www.shapenbarefoot.com/en/c/feelin/feelin-uni-mint-vegan-barefoot-sneakers).
  A retailer copy of the same model says "handcrafted in Portugal".
- Started 2017 (first e-shop 2019; Wayback from 2019-12).
- Could qualify if most of the range turns out to be Portuguese-made. Ask the brand
  for the split (see `docs/outreach.md`).
