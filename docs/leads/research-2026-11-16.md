# Research pass, 2026-11-16

Four brands suggested by the maintainer. Curel and Ementa were published; Bontön and
Salgueiro Home stay out.

## curel (new, published)

Verdict: strong.

- "Fazemos facas, canivetes e utensílios de cozinha em Portugal, nas nossas
  instalações de cerca de 9.000 m²." and "ganha rigor na produção industrial e é
  acabada e afiada à mão pelos nossos mestres cutileiros." —
  https://curel.pt/sobre/
- "9000 m² de fábrica em Caldas da Rainha." and "Zona Industrial, Santa Catarina.
  Aberta a visitas." — https://curel.pt/
- Independent: the Ambiente 2025 exhibitor listing (Messe Frankfurt) gives the same
  Zona Industrial nº 26, 2500-773 Santa Catarina address —
  https://ambiente.messefrankfurt.com/frankfurt/en/exhibitor-search.detail.html/curel---indstria-de-cutelarias-lusitana-lda.html ;
  the Centro de Portugal tourism board names Curel among the cutlers of Santa
  Catarina — https://www.centerofportugal.com/article/cutlery-in-caldas-da-rainha-and-alcobaca
- Company: the site says "Cutelarias Curel, Lda."; the fair listing and empresite say
  "CUREL - Indústria de Cutelarias Lusitana, Lda.". To check.
- "desde 1977" is the brand's own claim; `founded` left empty until a second source
  backs it.
- The factory is open to visits, but no factory shop is listed, so no
  `physical_stores`.

## ementa (new, published, parcial)

Verdict: weak (label only), corroborated by press. Held in October because only one
season was described as made in Portugal (`sources-2026-10.csv`).

- Shopify catalogue, 2026-11-16: 298 of 402 products say "Made in Portugal" (two
  "Handmade in Portugal"); none names another country. Unlabelled: about 20 caps,
  some shirts, jackets and T-shirts, skate decks, jewellery, gift cards.
- Time Out Lisboa, 2017-12-28: "and they’re all produced in Portugal." —
  https://www.timeout.com/lisbon/shopping/ementa-sb
- "Ementa was created in Lisbon back in 2007" — https://ementasb.com/pages/about-us-1 ;
  a retailer says 2006 in Amadora, so `founded` is left empty.
- No factory or workshop named, so no `location`.
- Shops from https://ementasb.com/pages/store-locations-1 , all with daily opening
  hours: LX Factory, Chiado, Boavista (Lisboa), Sá da Bandeira (Porto). The Paris
  shop (11 Rue Montmartre) is abroad and left out.

## bonton (not listed)

Verdict: contradicts. No production claim anywhere; the about page only says "a
newly created Portuguese brand". The shop's own Shopify `vendor` field names its
suppliers: AliExpress, "Factory Price Jewelry", "Zhangjiagang City Daking
Jewellery", "Sea Shells Supplies Philippines", "Antik Istanbul" and others; 5 of 274
products list Bontön itself. A reseller, not a lead.

## salgueiro-home (not listed)

Verdict: nothing beyond a curtain workshop. M. A. Salgueiro, S.A. (Espinho, since
1974) "iniciou a sua atividade comercializando tapeçarias orientais" —
https://salgueirohome.com/pt/about-us/ ; its registered activity is wholesale of
furniture and rugs. Only "um atelier de confeção de cortinas" is its own production;
no page says where the rugs or furniture are made. Lead at most.
